How it works
  • When the projector shows Question 7, tap 7 on your phone.
  • First, read the figure and the question on the projector. The answer options stay locked.
  • When your lecturer releases the options, a 2-digit code appears on the projector. Type it to see the options.
  • You then have 30 seconds. When the time ends, that question locks.
  • Your options are in a different order from your friends’ options.
⚠ Anti-cheating rules
  • Stay on this page: no other tabs or apps, no split screen, no notifications. Keep your screen on.
  • Each time you leave, your options are shuffled again. You get 2 warnings; the 3rd time, your quiz is submitted automatically.
